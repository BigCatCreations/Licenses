All stages of the below work will be completed in a way that they are compliant with the design brief. At the end of each stage, the licensee may decide to end further development if they choose to work with another partner.

### STAGE 1 // PLANNING
1. Gathering of detailed references of gothic architectural elements, relevant floorplans, fantastical elements, crystal structures, wisteria, crawling vines, and lunar elements
2. Development of a detailed set of floorplans and concept drawings to guide the 3D modelling of the venue

**DELIVERABLE:** Copies of high resolution watermarked scans of drawings and plans
### STAGE 2 // INITIAL PROTOTYPE
1. Grey box block out of the venue that can be explored on VR Chat to guide refinement of the layout and the relationships between spaces

**DELIVERABLE:** FBX of grey boxed venue

**NOTE:** Room count & layouts are finalized at the end of Stage 2 
### STAGE 3 // DEVELOPMENT
1. Development of texture atlases, optimized meshes, and graphics needed for the castle
2. Creation of a well directed world lighting experience
3. Implementation of existing scripts and systems to provide initial functionality
4. Development of new scripts and systems needed to complete the world's functionality
5. Animation of world features to bring life

**DELIVERABLE:** World privately uploaded to Amy's account that can be explored and used for test events or parties.
### STAGE 4 // Methodical Optimisation & Quest Port
1. Using tools like the Unity Profiler and Frame Debugger performance limitations will be identified and addressed
2. World download size will be targeted to be <125 MB

**DELIVERABLE:** World privately uploaded to Amy's account, as well as other requested accounts. A copy of the Unity Project will be provided, excluding software that requires the licensee to purchase a personal license (e.g., Bakery)

**NOTE:** World size target is "large" due to the scale of planned venue. 
### STAGE 5 // SEASONAL THEMING
1. Sets of decorations for Halloween, Christmas, Valentines will be developed and implemented within the world
2. Snow toggle
3. Implementation of a way of having plants have seasons

**DELIVERABLE:** Updated version of the uploaded worlds, and Unity Project Files


## Design Brief
### Theming
- Gothic architectural theming ([https://www.britannica.com/art/Gothic-architecture](https://www.britannica.com/art/Gothic-architecture "https://www.britannica.com/art/Gothic-architecture")) with fantasy elements
	- Pointed Windows, flying buttresses, ornate columns, rib vaulted ceilings, & stained glass windows
	- Dark masonry, silver accents, with hints of wood
- Faire magic making itself present through magical floating particles and clearly being the source of how things like floating candles exist
- Purple & Teal Crystals that are integrated into the architecture, and pulsing with magic
- Greenery that is living as a part of the castle like wisteria, and crawling vines
- Lunar themes throughout the design
- Lighting
  - Warm light from fireplaces, and candles
  - Moonlight streaming in through the windows
  - Cold teal and purple light coming from the crystals

### Required Rooms
- Lobby Space
- Reception Room
- Grand Hall
	- Grand room that serves as a central node of the building
	- Flanking staircases that lead up to another level --> not built out fully, but implied
	- Straight through leads to the primary ball room
	- To the right leads towards a hall that has balconies outside of it, which is attached to a lounge and bar
	- To the left is the throne room
	- Court yard with a wisteria tree at the center
- Primary Ballroom
	- Amphitheatre seating
	- Multi-purpose floor at the bottom (for dancing, parties, etc...)
	- Cabaret Stage
	- VJ Booth looking down to the stage
	- Suggestion: Toggleable DJ booth that can go on the stage
	- Connected to staff room
	- Easy access to the bar to get drinks
- Bar
- Sitting Rooms
	- Cozy sitting areas throughout the castle
	- Quiet spaces to get away from the noise or energy of the party if needed
- Throne Room
- Chapel

### Required Systems
- Video Player --> https://github.com/vrctxl/VideoTXL
- Whitelist System  --> Custom implementation of TXL Access
- AudioLink --> https://github.com/llealloo/audiolink
- VR Stage Lighting (VRSL) --> https://github.com/AcChosen/VR-Stage-Lighting
- VJ Systems (stage effects and directable lighting)
- Customizable banner systems
	- I have two ideas right now on how this could be approached, but it requires a touch of investigation to find a *good* solution.
- World Theming
	- Different seasons are likely achieved in a far more optimized way by having multiple world upload options.
	- I will however look into ways of making some of these things controlled via scripts, as that may be sufficiently performant
